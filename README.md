# The Frame of Photographs

### A Creative Archive of Visual Representation, Identity, and Otherness

This archive presents a curated collection of my professional photographs, editorial portraits, photoshoots, and visual compositions.

Through photographic representation, this collection documents my engagement with visual expression, artistic identity, and the ways individuals are presented and perceived through images.

## About This Collection

The archive brings together selected photographs from my professional creative practice as an artist and model.

## Collection

- Professional Photoshoots
- Editorial and Fashion Portraits
- Artistic and Conceptual Photographs
- Selected Visual Compositions

## Artistic Interests

- Visual Representation
- Identity and Self-Presentation
- Photography and Visual Culture
- Aesthetics and Composition
- Representation and Perception
